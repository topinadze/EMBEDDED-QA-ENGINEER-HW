Precondition:
- A finite `distance 3` check returned 28.0 cm for all three readings.
- Guard was DISARMED; zone was changed from 50 cm to 20 cm with prior operator approval.
- The target remained in place; subsequent sonar reading drifted to 19.2 cm.

- The countdown expired and the device entered GENERAL ALARM.

Alarm effects and recovery:

- Further ALARM-active reminders appeared about 10 seconds apart.
- The incident also emitted CIC messages for: INFO/watch (arming), WARNING/sonar (collision risk), WARNING/watch (intrusion), CRITICAL/watch (general alarm), and WARNING/telemetry (watch alarm). Alert identifiers and timestamps are omitted.
- A later disarm using the operator's local PIN succeeded. Guard reported DISARMED and the valve CLOSED.
- Immediately after disarm, the supplied Guard readback was DISARMED with zone 20 cm.
- Cleanup was subsequently completed: `guard zone 50` was accepted and a `guard` readback confirmed DISARMED, zone 50 cm.
- The supplied sequence does not include a physical LED observation. Alerts were not acknowledged as part of this check.
- The CIC alert messages are observed at generation time only. This sequence did not run `alert list`, verify pending markers/retention, or acknowledge alerts.

Assessment:
- The arming countdown/ARMED transition, threshold-triggered intrusion, 15-second response window, timeout-to-ALARM path, CRITICAL/valve/state effects, reminder cadence sample, and disarm-from-ALARM/valve-close behavior were observed.
- The intended clean arm-then-disarm-before-timeout scenario did not pass because the target measurement crossed the threshold and the first disarm command omitted its required argument.
