Test: HL-03 automatic-reporting off-state NVS persistence
Requirement: HL-3
Firmware: 2.3.0 (ota_0)
Capture date: 2026-10-21
Capture source: operator-provided UART excerpts; credentials and account/network/device identifiers omitted.

Pre-check:
- Device reported firmware 2.3.0 / ota_0 and state NORMAL.
- Initial health readback was auto on, interval 30 s.
- The initial console had no Wi-Fi connection or login. Saved-profile reconnect and local authentication were completed before changing the setting; health then read back auto on/30 s.

Disable and pre-reboot readback:
TX > health off
RX < [TELEMETRY] auto report off
TX > health
RX < [TELEMETRY] auto off, interval 30 s (now 30 s in NORMAL)

Reboot and post-reboot readback:
- One software reboot completed; device returned on 2.3.0 / ota_0 in NORMAL.
- Wi-Fi reconnect and local authentication were completed after reboot.
TX > health
RX < [TELEMETRY] auto off, interval 30 s (now 30 s in NORMAL)

Restore:
TX > health on
RX < [TELEMETRY] auto report on
TX > health
RX < [TELEMETRY] auto on, interval 30 s (now 30 s in NORMAL)

Result:
- The auto-off setting persisted across the tested software reboot at a 30 s interval.
- Automatic reporting was restored and read back as on/30 s.
- This result covers one software reboot only; it does not establish OTA persistence or repeated-reboot behavior.
