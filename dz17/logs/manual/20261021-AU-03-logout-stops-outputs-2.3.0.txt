Test: AU-03 logout stops active lamp, relay, and sonar stream
Requirement: AU-3
Firmware: 2.3.0 (ota_0)
Capture date: 2026-10-21
Capture source: operator-supplied UART excerpts and physical observations; credentials and identifiers omitted.

Precondition:
- 2.3.0 on `ota_0`, NORMAL, Wi-Fi connected, user logged in, Guard disarmed.
- Lamp off, relay closed, relay automation off, and no stream active.
- A stationary target was placed approximately 30 cm from the sonar; operator confirmed relay actuation was safe.

Observed:
- `lamp on` returned `[BEACON] on`; operator reported the lamp visibly illuminated.
- `relay on` returned `[VALVE] OPEN`; operator reported physical relay/valve actuation.
- `distance log 1000` ran for approximately 10 seconds and emitted repeated 28.0 cm readings.
- `logout` reported the session ended, lamp off, valve closed, and sonar pinging stopped.
- No further sonar readings appeared after logout during the operator's subsequent observation window (the next supplied line was an unrelated ACU message about 29 seconds after the final ping).
- Operator reported the lamp went out and the relay/valve closed.
- `status` confirmed NORMAL, Wi-Fi/internet still up, user not logged in, valve closed, and Guard disarmed. `whoami` returned `not logged in`.
- A second `logout` returned W211 `not logged in`.

Assessment:
- PASS for the observed AU-3 output/stream cleanup and repeated-logout W211 behavior in NORMAL.
- Logout from DEBUG/VERBOSE was not part of this run; those state-transition cases are covered separately under ST-4.
